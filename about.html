Founder & Director

Elizabeth Tengbeh

Founder • CEO of Beauty Academy • Professional Makeup Artist

Elizabeth Tengbeh is the visionary founder and director of Legacy Beauty Academy. With years of hands-on experience as a professional makeup artist, she established the academy to provide structured, industry-relevant training that prepares students for real-world success.

Under her leadership, the academy offers professional training, certification programs, masterclasses & workshops, portfolio development, and bridal & event makeup services.
